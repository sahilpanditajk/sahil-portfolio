Byline

Cross-platform journalist, trained on deadline.

I currently work as a News Desk Reporter & Anchor at Take One Digital Network, one of J&K's prominent news and media platforms, where I research, write, edit and present news across web, bulletin and social formats. Alongside this, I serve as an empanelled Resource Person with Doordarshan Kendra Jammu's Regional News Unit, writing and structuring broadcast-standard bulletins for Doordarshan.

My editorial training began at Daily Excelsior, J&K's leading English daily, where I proofread and wrote news pages, features and bylined articles under strict print deadlines — including pieces on National Parents' Day and the Kargil War Anniversary.

I'm currently pursuing an M.A. in Journalism & Digital Media at IGNOU, building on a B.C.A. from Cluster University and an Advanced Diploma in Computer Applications — a technical foundation I now put to work in digital publishing, SEO and newsroom tools.
