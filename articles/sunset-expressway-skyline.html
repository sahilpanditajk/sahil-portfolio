Photo Essay Near Suicide Point, Jammu

Where the Sunset Meets the Skyline of Tomorrow

By Sahil Pandita

Setting sun casts a golden glow over the under-construction Delhi–Amritsar–Katra Expressway as it sets near Suicide Point, Nagrota, Jammu. | Photo: Sahil Pandita

The evening sun casts a golden glow over the lush valleys of Jammu as the under-construction Delhi–Amritsar–Katra Expressway stretches across the landscape on massive elevated pillars. Photographed from the popular Suicide Point viewpoint, the image captures workers continuing construction high above the valley while the surrounding forests, farmland and seasonal watercourse remain bathed in the warm colours of sunset.

The expressway, one of North India's key infrastructure projects, is expected to improve connectivity to Jammu and Katra, boost tourism, facilitate trade and reduce travel time between major cities. Framed against a peaceful natural backdrop, the photograph highlights the coexistence of environmental beauty and engineering ambition, illustrating how development is gradually reshaping the region without overshadowing its scenic charm.
